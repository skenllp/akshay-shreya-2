Our Journey

Our Love Story

Six Chapters, One Beautiful Journey

  1. Chapter 01 · 2022

    Where It All Began

    “Some beautiful stories begin unexpectedly.”

    In 2022, we met while pursuing our MBA together. What began as two students sharing classrooms, assignments, and everyday college moments slowly turned into a beautiful friendship.

  2. Chapter 02 · 2022–2024

    The Beautiful College Days

    “Two years, countless memories.”

    Between lectures, conversations, laughter, and unforgettable college experiences, our friendship grew stronger. Those two years gave us more than an education — it gave us a connection that would become an important part of our lives.

  3. Chapter 03 · 2024

    Different Cities, One Connection

    “Life took them in different directions.”

    After completing our MBA, Shreya moved to Chennai for work, while Akshay began his career in Kerala. Our college days had ended, and a new chapter of responsibility, ambition, and distance had begun.

    Akshay in Kerala · Shreya in Chennai

  4. Chapter 04 · 2024–2025

    Beyond the Distance

    “Some bonds only grow stronger with time.”

    For the next year, we followed our professional journeys in different cities. Despite the physical distance and changes in our everyday lives, the connection we had built during college remained meaningful.

    2024–25a year, two cities
  5. Chapter 05 · 2025

    Choosing Each Other

    “Some decisions are written by the heart.”

    After a year of building our separate careers, we made one of the most meaningful decisions of our lives — to walk forward together. Our friendship had grown into a relationship we wanted to cherish for a lifetime.

  6. Chapter 06 · Our Forever

    A New Beginning

    “From MBA classmates to forever partners.”

    What started in a classroom in 2022 has become a beautiful journey towards forever. Through friendship, memories, different cities, and important life decisions, we are now ready to begin our next chapter together.
